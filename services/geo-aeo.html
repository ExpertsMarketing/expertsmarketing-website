FAQ about GEO

What clients ask before starting.

How does GEO integrate with SEO, paid media, and content strategy?

AI visibility should not operate separately from acquisition strategy. The strongest results come from aligning SEO, paid media, content, and GEO around the same commercial topics, audiences, and conversion objectives.

How long does it take to improve AI visibility?

Some improvements can appear within weeks after structural optimization and content updates. However, building strong AI authority and citation consistency is a medium-term process requiring ongoing optimization and content reinforcement.

How do you measure your GEO success?

Share of voice across the major AI engines for the questions that matter to your category, plus citation rate (how often your domain appears as a source) and AI-referred traffic where it's measurable.

Why work with Experts Marketing for GEO / AEO?

We combine 20 years of international SEO and acquisition experience with hands-on AI visibility optimization. Our approach is execution-focused. For example, restructuring content for the French market of Indeed helped increase their AI citation frequency on career-related search queries by 5%.

What's the minimum GEO engagement?

Three months to set the foundations and see early citation movement. Six to nine months to evaluate share-of-voice change properly.
