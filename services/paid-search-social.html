FAQ

What people ask before starting.

What's the minimum monthly spend?

We typically start engagements at €15k/month media spend or above. Below that, the math on agency fees vs. in-house rarely works in the client's favor.

Do you produce creative?

Yes. Creative production cadence is part of every engagement — usually a mix of motion graphics, static and UGC. We can also slot into existing creative teams when that's the better fit.

How do you measure incrementality?

Geo holdouts, brand search lift, MMM where budget allows. We're skeptical of last-click reporting and we'll say so on day one.

Can we run paid alongside SEO?

Strongly recommended. Paid tells us what converts, SEO defends those terms organically. The two share data and feed each other.

What's the contract length?

Three-month minimum, then month-to-month. We don't lock clients into long contracts — if the work isn't producing, you should be free to leave.
