How engagements evolve

Engagements move through three phases and stay on whichever one keeps producing — there's no fixed end date on growth.

Foundations & quick wins

FAQ about SEO

What clients ask before starting.

How do you align SEO with conversion and lead generation?

We optimize content and landing pages around commercial intent, not only traffic volume. The objective is to attract users more likely to convert into leads or customers.

Do you manage international and multilingual SEO?

Yes. We work with many global companies for various markets such as Microsoft, Tenable or Indeed. We have experience optimizing SEO performance across multiple markets and languages including English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese, and Chinese.

Do you work with our existing content team?

Yes. Many clients already have an editorial team — we plug into that with briefs, technical guidelines, and review. Other clients use our editorial network end-to-end. Both work.

How does AI impact SEO strategy today?

AI-driven search is changing how users discover brands and consume information. Our SEO approach integrates AI visibility optimization to strengthen both traditional search performance and visibility in AI-generated answers.

How do you measure SEO performance?

We focus on business-oriented KPIs such as: qualified organic traffic, non-branded search growth, conversion performance, lead generation, and visibility on strategic commercial queries.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

Technical and structural improvements can generate early visibility gains within a few weeks. More significant growth in rankings, qualified traffic, and authority typically builds over several months through continuous optimization.

What's the minimum engagement?

Three months for any meaningful work, six months to nine months to evaluate impact properly. We don't take on engagements where the timeline doesn't match the channel — it's not fair to either side.
