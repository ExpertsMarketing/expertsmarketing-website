FAQ

What people ask before starting.

Which platforms do you cover?

LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube as primary. We pick based on where your audience is and where the content format fits — not based on internal preference.

Do you produce video?

Yes — short-form motion is core to most engagements now. We work with our production network in Barcelona, or with your in-house team where you have one.

How do you measure organic social?

Engaged audience growth, share of voice in your category, and downstream signals like brand search and inbound demos. Not impressions or follower counts on their own.

Will you ghostwrite for our founder?

Often, yes. Founder-led content is one of the highest-leverage formats on LinkedIn — we partner closely with founders on tone and positioning, then handle production.

What's the minimum engagement?

Six months. Social compounds slowly — anything shorter doesn't give the work room to produce.
